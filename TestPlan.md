Play Test Questions:
1. How long did it take to complete the puzzles?
2. Was there too many, too few, or just the right amount of puzzles?
3. Is the map too big, too small, or just the right size?
4. Is there anything you feel like you still don't understand, even after finishing the game?
5. Was there anything that felt unbalanced (monster, puzzles, movement, etc).

A google form will be used to get information from players and that will also make it easier to record the results digitally. The form consists of a mix of multiple choice questions and short responses. Testers will be told to mostly focus on the puzzles and to maybe do certain things to attract  the monster to make sure that its movement works when noise is made. Some form of notes template will be used to document all the information gathered by the google forms responses.
